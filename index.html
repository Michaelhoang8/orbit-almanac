Energy change is divided by initial kinetic energy + |initial potential energy|, or 1 for a zero-energy isolated state. Momentum and center-of-mass errors use absolute dimensionless units. Peaks include every step, not just recorded frames.

Force law, numerical limits & interpretation

The model uses pairwise acceleration proportional to displacement / (r² + ε²)^(3/2), with G = 1. Potential energy uses −m₁m₂ / sqrt(r² + ε²). Softening changes the physical model and prevents a singular force at coincident positions. This is not a collision simulation.

Explicit Euler updates positions and velocities from the start of each step. Velocity Verlet averages start/end accelerations. RK4 evaluates four derivative stages. More accurate-looking plots are not sufficient evidence of accurate dynamics; compare diagnostics and repeat with a smaller time step.

Use 1–8 bodies, mass 0.001–100, initial coordinates within ±100, initial velocities within ±20, time step 0.0001–0.1, softening 0.001–1, and up to 20,000 steps. Runs stop if state magnitudes exceed one million or become nonfinite. There is no adaptive step size or collision response.

At most 501 frames per method are retained. Playback animates recorded samples, not wall-clock physical time. The circular binary is initialized for this softened force law; the three-body scene is an illustrative encounter rather than a claimed stable orbit. All work stays in your browser.
